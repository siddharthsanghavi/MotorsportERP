Roadmap

What's planned next. Every phase connects parts to more of the app so a part's whole story is in one place. Have an idea? Use 🐞 Report a bug and say it's a request.

Phase 1 · Part record as the hub

Next
  • Part statuses: In Design → Manufacturing → Ordered → In Inventory → Installed → Retired.
  • A part page that shows everything linked to it: project, drawings, jobs, purchases, spares, test history.
  • Responsible engineer on each part (defaults to the project POC) who gets part-update notifications.

Phase 2 · Design flows into purchasing or manufacturing

Planned
  • Design projects list the parts they own; PP1 creates them as In Design.
  • Each part is marked Make or Buy during design.
  • Passing PP3 sends every part on: Make parts become machining requests in Manufacturing, Buy parts become purchase requests in Purchasing (pre-filled with the vendor, budget and project).
  • Released drawings show on the part; a machining job can't pass CAM approval without one.

Phase 3 · Making and buying update parts

Planned
  • Finishing the manufacturing checklist sets the part to In Inventory (middle red cabinet).
  • Receiving a purchase sets its part to In Inventory; the part shows open and past orders.
  • Tooling used on a job counts down stock automatically.

Phase 4 · Money and spares

Planned
  • Actual spend per part, assembly and system, rolled up from purchases and funds (no typed-in costs).
  • Spares in stock link to their part number; low spares ping the responsible engineer.

Phase 5 · Testing closes the loop

Planned
  • The car log knows which parts are installed on that car; km are added per part.
  • Issues in the conclusion can be tagged to a part and become a task on its project.
  • Import an MCAP CSV export of logged data into the session info.

Later

Ideas
  • Data flow diagram of the whole app (React Flow) on this page.
  • Bring back part life tracking and a failure log once parts are linked.
  • Shared storage so the team works from one dataset instead of each browser.
