How to use MotorsportERP

One section per page. Data lives in this browser (localStorage), so back up regularly from Workspace ▾.

Overview

Open Overview
  • Counts across the team, Waiting on you (everything that needs your sign-off or action), and the audit log of who did what.
  • Use Acting as in the header to switch person and see what each role can do. Roles are enforced in the page only, not as real security.
  • The 🔔 bell holds your notifications. Workspace ▾ loads demo data, downloads or restores a backup (Admin), lists bug reports and clears the workspace.

Design

Open Design
  • Propose a design project at the bottom of the page. It starts as Proposed; the subteam lead starts PP1 or rejects it.
  • Each gate (PP1, PP2, PP3) is worked on, then submitted to the subteam review (subteam lead), then the chief review (Chief Mechanic, President, VP or Admin). Passing PP3 approves it for Manufacturing.
  • Either review can reject (a reason is required); the subteam lead can request changes and the chief can send it back to the subteam review. The POC can't sign off their own gate.
  • Cards show the project, its stage and the latest comments. Click Tasks or Team & details to open them. The subteam and POC names link to the Team page.
  • The Design timeline only shows what has happened so far: each bar is split into the stages the project has been through, up to today.

Parts

Open Parts
  • Click a car (KS10, KS10E, or add one), then a system (the FSAE cost-report systems), then an assembly, to see and add its parts. Each part belongs to one car.
  • Leave the part number blank and it's generated as car-system-number, e.g. KS10-BR-001.
  • Export … cost report BOM (CSV) (inside a car) gives the parts in the FSAE cost-report structure (system, assembly, part, quantity, material), without costs.
  • Search all parts finds a part on any car.

Assembly

Open Assembly
  • A graph for one car at a time: car → system → assembly → part. Pick the car at the top right.
  • Drag from an assembly's right handle to a part to move the part into that assembly (subteam leads).

Documents

Open Documents
  • Register a document (title, type, revision, subteam, link to the file); it gets a number like DOC-0001.
  • It goes Draft → In Review → Released. Authors can't release their own documents; New rev starts the next revision.

Manufacturing

Open Manufacturing
  • Anyone can request a part to be machined. The manufacturing lead is notified, assigns the machinist and machine (Edit), and the job follows the 7-step checklist (shown on each job card).
  • Only the manufacturing lead can tick fill out the job and CAM approval. The machinist ticks the rest. The last step notifies the requester, the part's subteam lead and the manufacturing lead (part-update).
  • Tooling & consumables: end mills, inserts, nuts and bolts with order links and an alternate vendor. Use counts them down; at the minimum the manufacturing lead is pinged and can Reorder.

Stock & tools

Open Stock & tools
  • Tools, tooling, consumables, spares and safety gear. Check tools out and in (some need training first), print QR labels, and scan labels with a USB scanner in the scan box.

Testing

Open Testing
  • Log a test session (car, event, driver, laps, km). Km add wear to every installed part on that car; issues can become tasks on a project.
  • The car log opens after you log a session (or click Car log on a session). Fill in the test plan, personnel (pick names from the dropdowns, several per role), conditions, car checks, the setup sheet before the run and the setdown sheet after, laps, timing sheet and conclusion. It saves as you type.
  • Copy setup from last session starts from the previous run's setdown; Copy from setup fills the setdown so you only change what moved.

Purchasing

Open Purchasing
  • Raise a request with the submit date, the budget it counts against and the fund it's paid from.
  • Purchasing Approvers move it through Requested → Quoted → Approved (over $500 needs the President, VP or Chief Mechanic). An Admin approves the funds before it can be Ordered.

Vendors

Open Vendors
  • The supplier list with contacts, lead times and spend. Purchases, stock and parts pick vendors from a dropdown; "+ Add a new vendor…" adds one on the spot.

Budget

Open Budget
  • Funding accounts (KSU Foundation, Fund 1300, Johnson, Department, Team total, PayPal…) show available, spent, committed and remaining. Admins and Budget Managers set balances.
  • Budget categories show spend from purchase requests only.

Team

Open Team
  • The roster, roles and subteams, the org map, and training sign-offs. Links from Design open a subteam (?subteam=) or a person.

Reporting a bug

  • Found something broken? Click the 🐞 button at the top of any page. Say what went wrong and what you were doing; the page is recorded for you.
  • Admins see the list under Workspace ▾ → Bug reports, set the status (Open, In progress, Fixed, Won't fix), export CSV, or open one as a GitHub issue. You're notified when yours is fixed.
